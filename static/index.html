Claim Verification & Property Recovery Guidelines

To ensure secure retrieval and prevent fraudulent claims, all property recovery follows institutional standards.

1. Mandatory Proof of Ownership

To claim any item (especially ID cards, phones, bags, or laptops), claimants must present their valid AFIT Student/Staff ID card and provide matching serial numbers, unique sticker descriptions, or unlock passcodes.

2. Designated Security Handover

Found items are formally deposited with designated campus authorities (Hall Wardens, ICT Security, or Main Gate Security Post). Handover requires signing the AFIT Property Logbook.

3. Unclaimed Property Policy

Per institution regulations, unclaimed perishable or general items remaining at the security desk after 30 days are transferred to the Central Provost Department for official auction or disposal.

4. Emergency Security Contact

For urgent reports of stolen or high-value items (laptops, motorcycles, sensitive documents), contact the AFIT Security Command Post immediately at ext. 4900 or security@afit.edu.ng.
